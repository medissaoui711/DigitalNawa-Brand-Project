Executive summary

DigitalNawa is described as a Founder-Led Digital Brand with a Branded House as its initial architecture. Its professional positioning is AI-Assisted Full-Stack Developer, Tech Educator, and Digital Product Creator. Core areas are Digital Products & Development, AI & Automation, and Digital Education. The concept is an interlocked D/N with rising pixels. The original visual reference is unavailable, and the SVG is documented as a geometric reconstruction; exact fidelity is NOT VERIFIED.

The five core palette HEX values are approved as values in the supplied brief and recorded under DN-DEC-006. Usage roles noted below come from a draft local visual-system document and are not separately evidenced as approved assignments.

WP02 theme, focus, functional, and component values; WP03 typography; and WP04 lockups remain candidates. This guide selects no final primary logo and makes no whole-product accessibility claim.

Chapter 02 — Brand Personality and Communication

All principles here are PROPOSED guidance, pending owner acceptance.

Proposed personality

  • Clear: explain functionality and lessons directly.
  • Curious: show useful AI and automation experiments without implying every experiment is production-ready.
  • Practical: connect explanations to steps, examples, and known limitations.
  • Credible: separate evidence, opinion, and unfinished work.
  • Generous: teach in a way that helps readers apply ideas independently.

Chapter 10 — Accessibility and Technical Quality

Requirements and checks, not a conformance statement:

  • Test actual text/surface pairs and UI boundaries separately against applicable contrast thresholds.
  • Pair color status with text, icon, or programmatic state.
  • Use semantic HTML, ordered headings, accessible names, keyboard-operable controls.
  • Declare language and direction, isolate mixed-direction runs, test focus and keyboard order in RTL/LTR.
  • Use logical CSS properties and test responsive reflow.
  • Provide useful alt text; use empty alt for decorative images.
  • Verify SVG titles, descriptions, paths, and external references.
  • XML validity does not prove visual correctness or logo fidelity.

Browser/assistive technology behavior, component contrast, focus, keyboard flow, responsive reflow, Arabic shaping, and production integration remain NOT VERIFIED. No blanket WCAG claim is made.
